My Top Personal Values

Top Personal Values My Business

Responsibility

1. Responsibility is the act of taking ownership of one actions, regardless of the consequences.

2. When I was growing up I was always taught to take responsibility for my actions and to understand the impact they have on others.

3. It is important to me because it builds trust and respect with others.

4. If people could not trust me, it would damage my relationships and hinder my ability to work effectively with others.

Respect

1. Respect is showing respect and consideration regardless of differences.

2. I was taught to treat others how I would like to be treated, and this has shaped my understanding of respect.

3. It is important to me because it fosters a sense of community and belonging.

4. If I did not show anyone respect, it would create a hostile environment and damage my relationships.

Accountability

1. Accountability is accepting responsibility for one's actions and decisions.

2. My parents instilled in me the importance of being accountable for my actions from a young age.

3. It is important to me because it promotes personal growth and integrity.

4. If I were not accountable for my actions, I would not learn from my mistakes and grow as a person.

Cooperation

1. Cooperation is the process of working together to the same end.

2. When I played hockey I learned the importance of teamwork and that everyone has a role to play in achieving a common goal.

3. It is important to me because it encourages people working together and the sharing of ideas from each other.

4. If I did not cooperate with others, it would hinder our ability to achieve our goals.
